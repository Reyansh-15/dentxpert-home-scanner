DENTXPERT · NOVEMBER 19 CLASSROOM PRESENTATION

A research concept for home oral-health imaging

Important: DentXpert is an early-stage research and design idea, not a working medical device. It has not been clinically validated, cannot diagnose dental conditions, and is not for sale.

The idea

Explore how future home imaging tools might help people communicate observations to qualified dental professionals. The goal is clearer communication, not replacing a dentist.

Five design directions

Compact Wand, SmartGrip, ScanDock, VisionStand and SmartCase are conceptual designs. VisionStand is the current direction for further digital exploration. None is a tested scanner.

What I built

I developed a research-stage website with design explanations, an illustrative concept film, and visible limitations. The film is a visualization, not evidence of medical performance.

What comes next

Study feasibility, improve accessibility, ask experts for feedback, and research the engineering and safety testing that a real device would require.

Key lesson

A promising healthcare idea needs responsible evidence, clear communication, safety review and expert validation before medical claims can be made.

Questions and feedback

What part of the idea is clearest? What safety or usability question should be investigated first?
