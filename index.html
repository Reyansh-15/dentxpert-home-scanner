THE IDEA, IN BRIEF

Technology with a clear role.

DentXpert explores ways to make household oral-health information more understandable while keeping professional care central.

01 / HOME

A household-use concept

The proposal pairs a reusable handheld scanner with a replaceable oral-contact component.

02 / INFORMATION

Reports made to be understood

Language support and progress review are proposed features, subject to development and validation.

03 / CARE

A starting point for questions

Any information is intended to support a conversation with a qualified dentist, not replace one.

!IMPORTANT INFORMATION

A proposed concept—not a validated medical device.

DentXpert has not been clinically validated. Its scanning technology, materials, and supported indicators require engineering development, appropriate safety testing, and clinical validation. It does not diagnose conditions or replace a professional dental examination.
