# DentXpert two-minute classroom pitch (draft)

Hello. My project is DentXpert Home Scanner, an early-stage idea exploring how home oral-health imaging might someday support clearer conversations with dental professionals.

Today, DentXpert is a research and design concept, not a working or clinically validated medical device. It cannot diagnose dental conditions, and it is not available for sale.

I explored five possible design directions: Compact Wand, SmartGrip, ScanDock, VisionStand, and SmartCase. Each investigates a different aspect of usability, handling, storage, or form factor. VisionStand is my current primary direction for further digital design.

I also developed a website that explains the idea, shows an illustrative concept film, and clearly describes the project's limitations. The film is a visualization, not a demonstration of real clinical performance.

My next steps are to improve the website's accessibility, study engineering feasibility, and investigate what kinds of expert review and safety validation a real product would require. No medical claims should be made without reliable evidence.

The main lesson from this project is that an interesting healthcare technology idea needs more than a design. It needs careful research, understandable communication, responsible testing, and input from qualified professionals.

Thank you.

## Classroom checklist
- Practice aloud and adjust to fit 120 seconds; this draft is not yet timed.
- Open the site and test the film beforehand; keep a screenshot or offline slide ready.
- Avoid claiming a scanner prototype, AI diagnosis, or medical approval exists.
- Record teacher questions and feedback after the presentation.
