Research journey

Progress means reducing uncertainty.

DentXpert’s most important outputs today are clearer questions, safer boundaries, and testable next steps—not promises about a future product.

i
Current stage: concept definition

There is no working device, clinical validation, regulatory approval, or product availability. This site does not replace professional dental care.

Evidence path

Each stage depends on expert review and results from the stage before it.

01

Now

Define the problem responsibly

Document user needs, intended boundaries, accessibility goals, risks, and alternatives.

02

Research gate

Investigate technical feasibility

Compare possible sensing approaches with engineers and subject-matter experts before selecting a direction.

03

Safety gate

Prototype for controlled testing

Only a future prototype could begin material, hygiene, usability, privacy, and electrical safety evaluation.

04

Evidence gate

Independent clinical study

Any medical claim would require appropriately designed studies and expert interpretation; none exist today.

05

Regulatory gate

Review before availability

Applicable approvals, manufacturing controls, and post-market responsibilities would need to be determined.

Open questions

The unknowns stay visible.

Feasibility, safe oral-contact materials, inclusive handling, understandable communication, data minimization, and regulatory classification all remain unresolved.
