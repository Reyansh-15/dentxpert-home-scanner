About the project

Curiosity, made responsible.

DentXpert is a student-led research and design exploration asking how future technology might make oral-health information easier to understand—while keeping evidence and professional care central.

i
Transparent by design

DentXpert has no working device, clinical validation, regulatory approval, or product for sale. This site does not replace professional dental care.

Mission

Explore boldly. Communicate carefully.

The project uses visual design to make a possible future tangible, then clearly separates that vision from what has actually been built or proven.

Principle one

Never hide the concept status

Visitors should understand the project’s early stage within seconds, on every page.

Principle two

Turn unknowns into a research plan

Safety, feasibility, accessibility, privacy, clinical evidence, and regulation are workstreams—not footnotes.

Project boundaries

A public concept site, not a care service.

01

No patient data

The website does not accept images, symptoms, contact details, or medical information.

02

No commercial offer

There are no purchases, subscriptions, preorders, or paid commitments.

03

No implied endorsement

The project does not claim expert, clinical, regulatory, or institutional approval.

04

No private health guidance

Questions about personal health belong with qualified professionals, not this site.
