General education

Start with the basics.

This page offers general context—not personalized advice, screening, monitoring, or diagnosis. A qualified dental professional is the right source for individual concerns.

i
Professional care comes first

DentXpert is a concept with no working device. It cannot detect, rule out, or monitor disease and does not replace professional dental care.

A simple care pathway

Notice. Care. Ask.

Visual information can be useful only when it is accurate, understandable, and connected to appropriate professional advice.

Every day

Maintain routines

Follow the oral-hygiene guidance given by your dental professional.

Regularly

Attend check-ups

Routine professional visits matter even when nothing feels wrong.

When concerned

Notice changes

Persistent pain, swelling, bleeding, or unusual changes deserve attention.

Next step

Ask a professional

Seek qualified care rather than relying on an image or unvalidated tool.

Important boundary

An image is not a diagnosis.

Interpretation depends on clinical context, examination, appropriate imaging, and professional judgment. If you have urgent or worsening symptoms, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
