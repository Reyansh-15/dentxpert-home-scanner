Five speculative directions

Different forms. Shared unknowns.

These industrial-design studies explore possible interactions and use contexts. They are not physical prototypes, medical products, or evidence of feasibility.

i
Research status

DentXpert is a concept only. There is no working device, clinical validation, regulatory approval, or product for sale. This site does not replace professional dental care.

VS

Current digital priority

VisionStand

A standing form designed to explore steadier positioning and guided visual feedback. Sensor choice, safety, cleaning, and usability are unresolved.

CW

Compact Wand

A small handheld study focused on reach and straightforward handling.

SG

SmartGrip

A grip-led direction exploring comfort and control across different hands.

SD

ScanDock

A docking idea centered on storage, protection, and readiness.

SC

SmartCase

A portable enclosure concept for organized transport and storage.

Evaluation lens

A promising shape is only the start.

01

Could it be cleaned safely?

Materials, oral-contact surfaces, and cleaning methods would need specialist evaluation.

02

Could different people use it?

Reach, dexterity, vision, sensory needs, and clear instructions must shape any future study.

03

Could it protect privacy?

A responsible design should minimize data and make consent understandable before capture.

04

Could it be technically feasible?

No sensing method has been selected or demonstrated. Engineering evidence comes before capability claims.
