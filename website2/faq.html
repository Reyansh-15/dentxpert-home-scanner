Clear answers

What DentXpert is—and is not.

These answers reflect the current early-stage research status. The short version: it is a design exploration, not a healthcare product.

i
Research concept only

There is no working device, clinical validation, diagnostic accuracy, regulatory approval, or product for sale. This site does not replace professional dental care.

Safety first

For personal concerns, speak with a qualified dental professional.

Can DentXpert diagnose a dental condition?

No. DentXpert cannot diagnose, screen, monitor, detect, or rule out any condition. It is an unbuilt concept.

Is there a physical prototype?

No working scanner has been built. The images and diagrams communicate industrial-design ideas only.

Has accuracy been tested?

No. There is no selected imaging system or validated analysis, so there is no diagnostic-accuracy claim to make.

Can I buy or preorder DentXpert?

No. Nothing is for sale, and there are no paid services, subscriptions, deposits, or preorders.

Is the concept film public?

No. It remains withheld until narration permissions and caption accuracy are verified. It would be illustrative, not proof of performance.

Does this website collect medical information?

No. There are no forms, accounts, uploads, symptom checkers, analytics inputs, or other personal or medical data collection features.

What should I do about pain, swelling, or another concern?

Seek advice from a qualified dental professional. An image, website, or unvalidated concept should not guide personal treatment decisions.
