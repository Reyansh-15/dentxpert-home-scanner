# DentXpert launch creative system

Purpose: invite people to explore and genuinely star the DentXpert Home Scanner research repository. DentXpert is an early-stage research and industrial-design concept, not a medical device or clinical service.

## Master campaign line

**A clearer view starts with a question.**

## Creative directions

1. **The question** — scanner render, slow orbit, copy: “What should a home scanner explain before it shows anything?”
2. **The concept** — close-up industrial design, copy: “Meet the scanner concept designed to be questioned.”
3. **The boundary** — dark frame with three honest labels: “No diagnosis. No clinical claim. Research only.”
4. **The repository** — animated code-to-interface transition, CTA: “Explore the open-source build.”
5. **The graph** — knowledge graph nodes forming DentXpert, CTA: “See the questions connect.”
6. **The process** — four beats: question, prototype, evidence, review.
7. **The unknowns** — floating research cards, copy: “The unknowns are part of the project.”
8. **The interface** — command-center interaction capture, CTA: “Inspect the prototype.”
9. **The student build** — founder-style narration: “I’m building DentXpert in public.”
10. **The future** — cinematic scanner silhouette, copy: “Future health technology should know its limits.”
11. **The comparison** — five concept directions, CTA: “Compare the ideas.”
12. **The accessibility test** — keyboard focus and responsive layouts, copy: “Clarity should work for more people.”
13. **The privacy frame** — local-first diagram, copy: “No personal or medical data collected.”
14. **The invitation** — repository page and discussion prompt, CTA: “Challenge the assumptions.”
15. **The star request** — direct close: “Star the repository if you want to follow the build.”

## Voiceover template

“DentXpert is an early-stage research and design concept exploring how future home oral-health interfaces might communicate visual information more responsibly. It is not a diagnosis, a clinically validated device, or a product for sale. Explore the prototype, inspect the questions, and star the repository if you want to follow the work.”

## Required end card

**DentXpert Home Scanner**  
Early-stage research and industrial-design concept  
Explore: https://github.com/Reyansh-15/dentxpert-home-scanner  
**Star the repository to follow the build.**

## Production rules

- Use original DentXpert visuals and generated artwork.
- Never invent testimonials, clinical accuracy, regulatory approval, availability, or outcomes.
- Never imply that starring guarantees access or influence.
- Keep captions on every video.
- Include a visible concept disclaimer in every creative.
- Use 1:1, 9:16, and 16:9 crops from the same visual system.
